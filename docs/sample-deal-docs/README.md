# Sample deal documents (fictional, for testing)

Every document here is made up for testing the app: every page is marked
"SAMPLE – FICTIONAL – FOR TESTING ONLY", and the parties, addresses and
figures are invented, chosen to be consistent with Model Fund I's sample
assets and leases (`docs/sample-leases/`). Each phase that reads documents
adds its set here (plan decision 15).

## Everything ties out (1.1.13)

Every sample document the app loads is written **from Model Fund I as the
app builds it**, so a document and the app never disagree. To rebuild:

1. `node scripts/sample-deal-docs/export_fund.cjs` writes
   `scripts/sample-deal-docs/fund.json` from `lib/sampleFund.js` (the function
   "Load Model Fund I" calls).
2. **Leases** (`docs/sample-leases`): `python3 scripts/sample-leases/generate.py`,
   `node scripts/sample-leases/pdf.cjs`, `python3 scripts/sample-leases/build_fund.py`.
   One lease per asset, for a tenant on its rent roll; the unit's SF, type,
   expiration, rent and escalation are read from `fund.json`. Three have an
   amendment that changes no economic term. The abstracts are sample
   abstracts (not sent to the CREparser connector).
3. **Fund and asset documents** (`deals/`): `python3 scripts/sample-deal-docs/deal_docs.py`
   reads every figure it shares with the app from `fund.json`, and the
   estoppel, tenant certificate and CAM reconciliation from the leases'
   `index.json`. Only facts the app does not hold are written in the
   generator (contract dates and deposits, lender covenants and reporting,
   the cap's rating, the IC conditions, the LPA's limits).
4. **Sale documents** (`dispositions/`): `python3 scripts/sample-deal-docs/disposition_docs.py`.
   Three sales **in progress** (Route 9 under contract; Harbor Light and Mill
   Creek with an offer): a BOV and an offer letter each, and Route 9's
   contract. The broker's value is the asset's latest mark, or its year-1
   NOI at a cap rate just inside the going-in cap. There is **no closing
   statement**, because Model Fund I holds no sold asset.
5. `node scripts/sample-deal-docs/print_html.cjs docs/sample-deal-docs/deals [name …]`
   (and `…/dispositions`) prints the PDFs.
6. `node scripts/sample-deal-docs/make_terms.cjs` reads each abstract into
   the fund with the app's own apply functions and writes
   `lib/sampleDocTerms.json`, which the sample fund then carries: the terms
   of the fund and asset documents, what each lease adds to its tenant's unit
   (start date, deposit, recoveries, "no options"), and each sale with its
   BOV, offer and contract. It refuses if a document disagrees with the app
   or moves a loan, equity or return. A lease's rent schedule is not copied
   to the unit (the model escalates the unit's rent itself); it must give the
   unit's rent on the acquisition date and grow at the unit's escalation.
7. `node scripts/sample-yardi/generate.cjs` writes the sample Yardi-style
   exports (`docs/sample-yardi`) from the same fund; copy the two construction
   files into `lib/sampleConstruction.js`.
8. `node scripts/sample-deal-docs/copy_public.cjs` refreshes the copies the
   app uploads.
9. When a document is rewritten or retired, add its file name to
   `lib/sampleDocChanges.json` under the next `SAMPLE_DATA_VERSION` (fund and
   asset documents, `sales`, `leases`), so the automatic load replaces the
   earlier copy in a firm's data.

`tests/node/sampleTieOut.test.cjs` fails on any difference; it has no
exceptions list. Documents that differ from the model on purpose (for the
tests of comparing and applying a document) live in `tests/fixtures` and are
never loaded into the app: the earlier fund and asset documents
(`deal-docs/`), leases (`sample-leases/`), sale documents with closing
statements (`dispositions/`), Yardi-style exports (`sample-yardi/`) and the
tenant credit set (`tenant-credit/`).
