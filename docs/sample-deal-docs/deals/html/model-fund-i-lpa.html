Limited Partnership Agreement – Model Fund I, L.P. (excerpt)

Article 1

This Limited Partnership Agreement governs Model Fund I, L.P.

Aggregate Commitments: $100,000,000.

The General Partner commits 5% of Aggregate Commitments.

Article 4. Fees and distributions

The Management Fee is 1.5% per year of Commitments during the Investment Period and of Invested Capital thereafter.

Limited Partners receive a Preferred Return of 9% per year.

The General Partner catch-up is 100% until it has received 20% of the profits distributed.

After the catch-up, distributions are split 80% to the Limited Partners and 20% to the General Partner until the Limited Partners have achieved a 12% IRR; then 70% and 30% until they have achieved an 18% IRR; and thereafter 60% and 40%.

Each split applies only to the distributions made within its tier, and no split applies retroactively to distributions already made.

Article 5. Term

The Investment Period is 2 years from the Final Closing.

The Partnership's term is 5 years from the Final Closing.

The General Partner may extend the term by up to 2 extensions of 1 year each.

Borrowings under a subscription facility may not exceed 80% of unfunded Commitments.

Article 6. Investment limitations

Section 6.2(a): No single Investment may exceed 20% of Aggregate Commitments.

Section 6.2(b): No single tenant may account for more than 10% of the Partnership's aggregate base rent.

Section 6.2(c): Investments in any one state may not exceed 35% of aggregate gross asset value.

Section 6.2(d): Investments in any one metropolitan area may not exceed 25% of aggregate gross asset value.

Section 6.2(e): The Partnership invests only in industrial and flex properties.

Section 6.2(f): Development and other non-core Investments may not exceed 15% of Aggregate Commitments.

Section 6.3(a): Aggregate leverage may not exceed 65% of aggregate gross asset value.

Section 6.3(b): Leverage on any single Investment may not exceed 75% of its value.
